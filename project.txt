Safety risks at telecommunication construction sites may arise from uneven terrain, improperly operated machinery, poorly stored materials, missing personal protective equipment, and unsafe worker behaviour. Although deep-learning-based computer vision offers an efficient means of identifying such risks, its performance is often constrained by the limited availability and diversity of labelled, sector-specific images. The participating student will develop and evaluate a novel generative image-based learning framework for enhancing the construction safety of telecommunication projects, termed TelecomSafe. TelecomSafe is designed to identify various potential risks on telecommunication construction sites with high effectiveness and robustness. Different from existing frameworks and software products, TelecomSafe is specifically developed for the telecommunication sector and relies on the latest computing techniques, including generative artificial intelligence (AI), deep-learning-based image processing, and information fusion. In particular, generative AI is introduced to address the scarcity of training data for telecommunication construction safety enhancement, and deep learning in conjunction with image processing and information fusion techniques can provide multiple dimensions to accurately appraise the potential risks on construction sites, covering terrain, machinery, materials, and workers.

This project is of a complex nature and requires close collaboration among team members. The participating students need to be very self-motivated and willing to learn new things with a substantial time commitment. Note that, throughout the entire project cycle, regular in-person meetings and discussions on campus are required between the participating students and supervisor; absence and remote working on the project are unacceptable.

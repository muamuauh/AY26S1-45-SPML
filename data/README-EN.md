## Target classes (phase 1)

| id | Class | Dimension | Meaning | Decision criterion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | `person` | workers | People on the construction site (full-body box) | Label when more than half of the body is visible; for occlusion, box the visible part |
| 1 | `helmet` | workers | Safety helmet | Only helmets worn on the head; not helmets on the ground or held in the hand |
| 2 | `no_helmet` | workers | Head without a safety helmet | Box the head; ordinary caps and headscarves count as no helmet |
| 3 | `vest` | workers | Hi-vis vest / high-visibility workwear | Box the upper-body part of the vest being worn |
| 4 | `no_vest` | workers | Upper body of a person without a hi-vis vest | Box the torso without a vest |
| 5 | `harness` | workers | Full-body safety harness (chest and leg straps) | Box the harness worn on the body; a lanyard only attached to the structure is not labelled |
| 6 | `machinery` | machinery | Construction machinery (excavators, cranes, loaders, rollers, dozers, mixers, dump trucks...) | Box the whole machine including extended booms and buckets |
| 7 | `vehicle` | machinery | Ordinary vehicles (cars, pickups, vans, ordinary trucks) | Vehicles not specific to construction |

## Datasets in detail

### `construction_site_safety` — Construction Site Safety (Roboflow Universe, version 30)

- **Source**: https://universe.roboflow.com/roboflow-universe-projects/construction-site-safety
- **Description**: Roboflow's curated construction-site safety dataset (version 30, original un-augmented images) with positive and negative helmet / vest classes (NO-Hardhat, NO-Safety Vest), people, and fine-grained machinery and vehicles. Closest match to the phase 1 target classes and the only source of machinery and vehicles.
- **Size**: planned 717 images (train 521 / valid 114 / test 82) ｜ local raw 717 ｜ after build 683
- **Format / local path**: yolo ｜ `data/raw/construction_site_safety`
- **Class mapping**: Hardhat→helmet, NO-Hardhat→no_helmet, Safety Vest→vest, NO-Safety Vest→no_vest, Person→person, Excavator→machinery, wheel loader→machinery, dump truck→machinery, machinery→machinery, SUV→vehicle, bus→vehicle, mini-van→vehicle, sedan→vehicle, semi→vehicle, trailer→vehicle, truck→vehicle, truck and trailer→vehicle, van→vehicle, vehicle→vehicle, Gloves→dropped, Ladder→dropped, Mask→dropped, NO-Mask→dropped, Safety Cone→dropped, fire hydrant→dropped
- **Fully annotated target classes**: person, helmet, no_helmet, vest, no_vest, machinery, vehicle (other classes get teacher pseudo-labels)
- **Licence**: CC BY 4.0
- **Citation**: Construction Site Safety dataset (version 30), Roboflow Universe Projects, https://universe.roboflow.com/roboflow-universe-projects/construction-site-safety
- **Download**: Roboflow API: workspace `roboflow-universe-projects` / project `construction-site-safety` (`python -m telecomsafe.data.download --only construction_site_safety`)
- **Role**: training · A core
- **Notes**: Checked 2026-09-29: the Kaggle mirror used at first is Roboflow version 28, whose training split is 2,605 mosaics made from 521 originals x 5 augmented copies (mosaic, cutout, crop, rotation...). That broke the "no augmentation" E1 control and made the rules treat people and machines in different mosaic tiles as adjacent. Replaced with the un-augmented version 30 (717 images).

## Building TelecomEval

1. `python -m telecomsafe.data.collect_open` — search Openverse, Wikimedia Commons (with subcategories), Flickr and DVIDS for openly licensed (CC0 / PD / CC BY / CC BY-SA) candidates; attribution is recorded automatically. Flickr and DVIDS need a key in `.env` and are skipped otherwise
   `python -m telecomsafe.data.collect_video` — frames from Creative Commons YouTube videos: only sharp frames showing a whole person (a head inside the person box), at most 4 per video
2. `python -m telecomsafe.data.screen` — counts people with E2, scores the scene with CLIP and writes `data/raw/t3_candidates/review.html`; mark each image Telecom / Near (power-line work, a separate subset) / Reject there, export, then run `python -m telecomsafe.data.screen --apply <exported file>`
3. `python -m telecomsafe.data.pseudo_label --weights runs/phase1/e2/weights/best.pt --images data/raw/t3_candidates/images --yolo-out data/raw/t3_candidates/prelabels` — pre-labels (E2 labels people and PPE, a COCO model adds vehicles)
4. `python -m telecomsafe.data.labelstudio serve` starts Label Studio (account in `.env`); in another terminal, `python -m telecomsafe.data.labelstudio push` imports the kept images and pre-labels (use `push --add` for images added later)
5. At http://localhost:8080, correct every image against the decision criteria above: check each pre-label and add missing boxes (harness and machinery are never pre-labelled); Skip unusable images
6. `python -m telecomsafe.data.labelstudio pull` — exports YOLO labels to `data/raw/telecom_eval/` and writes `subsets.csv` (telecom / near)
7. `python -m telecomsafe.data.freeze_eval --create` — freeze; read-only afterwards and shared by both phases
